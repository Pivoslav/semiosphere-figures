## Core claim (not yet measured on a multi-agent plate)

Default LLM-to-LLM exchange is **primary-system coupling** (shared tokenizer, shared pretraining geometry, theme-level paraphrase), not **Lotman bilingual relay** (shell-typed partial map with operators, prohibitions, residue).
