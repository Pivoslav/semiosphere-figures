Figure 1 · Wording habits: secret vs public (Question 1)

Technical: operator counts internal spr. 1206 vs embassy/UN.

Side-by-side counts for five wording habits in secret March 1983 memos (blue) and embassy and UN texts from the same weeks (green). Examples: putting “symposium” in scare quotes, calling a conference a “gathering,” labelling people as enemy actors. Scare quotes appear 24 times in the secret memo and zero times in the public texts. The same habits stay at zero in diaspora and mainstream press (see Figure 10).

The same body wrote two different stories for two different readers. Moscow was meant to hear hostility; foreign audiences got smoother language about the same events. Each text reads fine on its own. They just cannot be collapsed into one neutral version.
