Round-trip asymmetry (MA12)

Lotman's test, stated bluntly: if you translate out and back and get the same text, you are inside an artificial language (logic, not poetry). High recovery is evidence of transmission. Low recovery can mean a creative relation, or it can mean corruption. Thirteen corpus stretches, five conditions, temperature 0.0, reverse passes audited so the original never leaked into the return call.

Figure R1 · Recovery by condition

Mean token Jaccard vs the original (n = 13 each)

Observed order: R0 (1.000) > R3 cross-model (0.892) > R2 cross-tier (0.267) > R1 register (0.224) > R4 three trips (0.158). The preregistered order predicted register and tier would sit below identity; it did not predict that swapping 3B for 1B would land near the ceiling.

Figure R2 · Where the distance actually lives (with a caveat)

Loss of recovery relative to R0 = 1.000

Register change on one model (R1) costs 0.776 of recovery. Swapping 3B for 1B (R3) costs 0.109. That looks like "the instruction is the real boundary," until you notice both models are one Llama 3.2 lineage sharing a tokenizer. Near-ceiling round trips are what Lotman predicts inside one artificial language (Universe of the Mind, trans. Shukman, chapter on the three functions of the text). Raznostrukturnye generators are from Lotman, “Asimmetriya i dialog” (1983), printed p. 26 (rendering mine). MA15 across genuinely different pretraining is the experiment that would test the criterion; this run only illustrates it by accident. Full citations: journal §11, §14.

Figure R3 · Low recovery is not creation

Original (silence unit): Detailed drought-and-kulak denial in this window sits in the Ottawa embassy release, Novosti, and the Khmil UN statement, not in a matching domestic Pravda article.

After register round trip (R1): statements denying the drought and persecution of peasants (known as kulaks) were published in a Canadian newspaper called Novosti and by Ukrainian politician Khmil.

Jaccard 0.224 on this stretch. The archive's documented absence came back as a positive claim about a Canadian newspaper. Lotman's asymmetry test is necessary but not sufficient: add a licensing check (does new material appear in the corpus?) before calling low recovery "creative."

Figure R4 · Three trips, no fixed point

Mean Jaccard vs original across three register round trips

Drift decelerates (successive-iteration similarity rises) but only 1 of 13 stretches reached a fixed point against a preregistered threshold of 7. The instructive fixed point deleted scare quotes and the named course the memorandum is about. Convergence bought by erasing what made the document evidence is not recovery.

Limits: one model family, thirteen stretches, one plate. A high score can mark refusal to transform (verbatim echo on the reverse pass). Vacuous survival measures read 1.000 when no stretch contained the item type. Source: ma12_roundtrip_report.json in the thesis lab.
