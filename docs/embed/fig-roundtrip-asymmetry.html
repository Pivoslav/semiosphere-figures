Round-trip asymmetry (MA12)

Lotman's test, stated bluntly: if you translate out and back and get the same text, you are inside an artificial language (logic, not poetry). High recovery is evidence of transmission. Low recovery can mean a creative relation, or it can mean corruption. Thirteen corpus stretches, five conditions, temperature 0.0, reverse passes audited so the original never leaked into the return call.

Mean token Jaccard vs the original (n = 13 each)

Figure R2 · Where the distance actually lives (with a caveat)

Loss of recovery relative to R0 = 1.000

Loss relative to this stack's R0 (0.877): register change (R1) costs about 0.603; qwen→gemma on the reverse pass (R3) costs about 0.137. The Llama 3B/1B run (R0 = 1.000) showed 0.776 vs 0.109 and looked like "instruction beats weights" until both models proved to be one family. Cross-family qwen/gemma lowers R3 but does not collapse it to the register floor. MA15 tests four family dyads. Citations: journal §11, §14.

Figure R3 · Low recovery is not creation

Original (silence unit): Detailed drought-and-kulak denial in this window sits in the Ottawa embassy release, Novosti, and the Khmil UN statement, not in a matching domestic Pravda article.

After register round trip (R1): statements denying the drought and persecution of peasants (known as kulaks) were published in a Canadian newspaper called Novosti and by Ukrainian politician Khmil.

Jaccard 0.224 on this stretch. The archive's documented absence came back as a positive claim about a Canadian newspaper. Lotman's asymmetry test is necessary but not sufficient: add a licensing check (does new material appear in the corpus?) before calling low recovery "creative."

Figure R4 · Three trips, no fixed point

Mean Jaccard vs original across three register round trips

Drift decelerates (successive-iteration similarity rises) but only 2 of 13 stretches reached a fixed point against a preregistered threshold of 7 (qwen stack, 2026-10-02). The instructive fixed point deleted scare quotes and the named course the memorandum is about. Convergence bought by erasing what made the document evidence is not recovery.

Limits: thirteen stretches, one plate, open local stacks (qwen/gemma). A high score can mark refusal to transform (9/13 R3 rows echoed forward verbatim on 2026-10-02).
