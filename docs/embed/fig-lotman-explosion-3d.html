The dissertation tests whether Soviet-era talking points on famine commemoration transform predictably when they cross register boundaries - secret memo → embassy/Novosti → domestic news → diaspora/scholarly print - and whether that transformation is dated and measurable (Montreal 1983 as anchor, post-1983 explosion as qualitative rewire). These 3D models make Lotman’s spatial vocabulary falsifiable: they read from the same open JSON as Figures 1-12.

In plain terms

Two phrase-network maps stacked vertically. Lower plane = memos before 1983 (only two early files). Upper plane = memos after 1983 (fourteen files). Each dot = a wording-habit cluster; each line = those habits appear in the same memo. Red lines = co-occurrences that did not exist before Montreal - new internal wiring. Grey vertical pillars = topics that continue across the rupture at the same horizontal position.

What connections tell us

Before 1983, three topics co-occur in a tight triangle - operational vocabulary, OUN actor references, scare quotes. After 1983, new nodes appear («artificial famine», hostile «gathering») and new edges link them. The institution did not merely write more memos; it rewired which phrases travel together when commemoration proceeds despite counter-measures. That is Lotman’s explosion as a dated, countable claim.

Thesis significance

Lotman’s ‘explosion’ names qualitative reshaping, not louder word counts. After Montreal 1983 the memo phrase network gains new nodes («artificial famine», hostile «gathering») and new edges between them - the institution rewires which wording habits co-occur when the outside story ‘wins.’ The lower plane is the pre-rupture bundle; red upper-plane edges are co-occurrences that did not exist before.

Connections on the upper plane are internal to the fond - they do not prove outward press changed line-by-line. They prove the secret register’s phrase ecology bifurcated after the 1983 anchor. Pair this figure with L1 (outward relay) and L2 (operator skew) for the full argument: boundary non-equivalence at the shell edge and qualitative rewire inside the archive after the outside story wins.
